## 🇨🇳 中文

面向想制作超写实真人未来科幻短片的创作者。用户提供故事梗概、人物或场景参考图、时长、段落数、画幅和风格偏好，流程先锁定创意简报与视觉方向，再建立人物、物种、道具和场景资产，制作连续性分镜，分段生成视频并按时间轴装配成片。人物和物种各自单独出纯白底资产卡，场景先出空镜单图再出 4K 四视图，分镜做到一段剧情对应一个 shot 和一个视频段落。默认 16:9 横屏，缺时长时按 60–90 秒、4–6 段、每段 10–15 秒执行。适合单条叙事科幻短片，不用于二次元、赛博朋克主导、音乐视频、片头标题序列或简单图生视频。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入 100–200 字梗概 → 输出严格时间轴：60 秒内 4–5 段、90 秒项目 6–10 段，每段最长 15 秒
- 每个角色与物种单独生成纯白底 9:16 资产卡，确认后合排 16:9 四视图（胸部近景/正面/侧面/背面）
- 场景先出 16:9 空镜单图，确认后再出 4K 2×2 四视图（正面中心线/左 45°/右 45°/最深处）
- 六段项目每段附一张十二宫格分镜图，逐格编号展示节拍、动作、镜头推进与段间衔接
- 生成前可选「先生成 shot 1 预览」；生成失败只针对报错原因重试失败段落，不动已确认模块
- 提示词固定按主体引用→场景空间→动作情绪→摄影机→光线介质→风格→约束编译，每段最多 9 张参考图

## 🇬🇧 English

For creators who want a hyper-real live-action future sci-fi short from a story brief. You supply a synopsis, optional character or scene references, duration, paragraph count, aspect ratio, and style preference; the workflow locks the brief and visual direction, builds human, species, prop, and scene assets, cuts a continuity storyboard, generates timed video segments, and assembles the approved film. Every character and species gets its own pure-white asset card, scenes start as an empty 16:9 still before a 4K four-view turn, and one plot paragraph maps to exactly one shot and one video segment. It defaults to 16:9 and, with no duration given, to 60–90 seconds in 4–6 segments of 10–15 seconds. Best for one narrative sci-fi short, not for anime, cyberpunk-first pieces, music videos, title sequences, or simple image-to-video motion.

### ✨ What it does

- A 100–200 word synopsis becomes a strict timeline: 4–5 segments under 60s, 6–10 for 90s, never over 15s each
- Each character and species gets its own pure-white 9:16 asset card, then one 16:9 four-view sheet (close-up, front, side, back)
- Scenes start as a 16:9 empty still, then a 4K 2×2 four-view set: center-front, left 45°, right 45°, deepest point
- Six-segment projects get one numbered twelve-panel storyboard per segment for beats, action, camera push, and hand-off
- Optional "generate shot 1 first" preview; a failed segment is retried once for the diagnosed cause without touching approved assets
- Prompts compile in a fixed order — subject reference, scene, action, camera, sourced light, style, constraints — with up to 9 reference images per segment
